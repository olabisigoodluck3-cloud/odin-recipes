JOLLOF RICE AND CHICKEN

DESCRIPTION

Jollof rice is the most common party food in africa,Nigeria most especially it is delicious,inviting and family friendly

ingredients

  • rice
  • fresh tomatoes
  • red bell peppers(tatashe)
  • scotch bonnet(ata rodo)
  • onions
  • tamato paste
  • vegetable oil
  • chicken
  • curry powder and thyme
  • bay leaf
  • salt and seasoning cubes

cooking steps

  1. blend the tomatoes,peppers and onions
  2. heat oil in a pot
  3. add sliced onions
  4. fry until light golden
  5. add tomato paste and fry for five minutes
  6. pour in the blended pepper
  7. add curry,thyme,bay leaf,seasoning cubes and salt
  8. cook for ten minutes, stirring occasionally
  9. pour in chicken
  10. taste and adjust seasoning
  11. add washed rice
  12. stir once
  13. cook on low heat for 20-30 minutes
  14. stir gently from bottom
  15. add butter or a little oil
  16. cook for another five minutes and dish ......bon appetite
