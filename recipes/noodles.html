NOODLES

DESCRIPTION

noodles is a fast food common in the whole world, either rich or poor. it is a food we cook when we are too tired to cook something heavy. but it is very delicious

ingredients

  • Noodles
  • water
  • salt
  • oil
  • vegetables (carrots, cabbage, and so on)
  • boiled egg

cooking steps

  1. wash and cut vegetables
  2. boil water in a pot
  3. add pinch of salt
  4. add noodles
  5. stir gently to prevent sticking
  6. cook until soft
  7. add carrots,peas etc
  8. add cabbage
  9. stir gently
  10. use a colander to drain water
  11. shake gently
  12. serve
  13. pour any sauce you desire......enjoy
